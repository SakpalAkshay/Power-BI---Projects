# Defining Hate Crime

## What It Is — and What It’s Not
A **hate crime** refers to **any incident**, whether it constitutes a crime or not, **that is perceived by anyone to be motivated by hostility or prejudice** based on a personal characteristic.

## Types of Hate Crime
- **Race**
- **Religion**
- **Sexual Orientation**
- **Gender Identity**
- **Disability**

---

## What Forms Can Hate Crime Take?
Hate incidents and hate crimes can appear in many ways, including:

- **Physical Violence**
- **Bullying**
- **Persistent Victimization**
- **Exploitation**

---

## Facts & Data
According to **Dr. Russell Jeung**, a founding member of **Stop AAPI Hate**:

- During 2020, **hundreds of hate incidents** were being reported daily.
- From **March to December 2020**, there were **over 2,800 cases** of hate incidents against Asian Americans across the United States.
- Hate crimes of this nature **continue to occur across the U.S.**

---

## What Victims Can Do
If you or someone you know experiences a hate incident:

1. **Report to school authorities.**
2. **Contact the police.**
3. **Seek help from professional support groups.**
